The Best Parks in Philadelphia

From a 2,000-acre riverside wilderness to hammock-strung waterfront pop-ups, Philly's green spaces are some of the best in any big city.

GPBy the GuidePhilly team · Updated July 2026
Advertisement728 × 90 · Monetag zone

For a dense East Coast city, Philadelphia is remarkably green, ringed and threaded by one of the largest urban park systems in the country.

Here are the parks, trails and gardens locals actually use, whether you want a rugged hike or a hammock by the river.

1

Fairmount Park

The city's sprawling 2,000-acre green heart

City ParkNorthwest PhiladelphiaFree
Fairmount Park

This massive riverside park unfurls over 2,000 acres of rolling hills, meadows and waterfront, threaded with miles of trails and dotted with historic mansions and public sculpture. It also serves as home base for beloved attractions like the Philadelphia Zoo, Shofuso Japanese Cultural Center and the Please Touch Museum.

Address: 1800 Belmont Mansion Drive, Philadelphia, PA 19131

2

Wissahickon Valley Park

Rugged forested gorge with 50-plus miles of trails

TrailNorthwest PhiladelphiaFree
Wissahickon Valley Park

A wooded ravine carved by Wissahickon Creek offers more than 50 miles of rugged paths for hiking, trail running, mountain biking and horseback riding just minutes from the city. Its centerpiece is Forbidden Drive, a flat five-mile gravel route that traces the creek past the historic Valley Green Inn and Philadelphia's last covered bridge.

Address: Valley Green Road, Philadelphia, PA 19128
Visit website ↗Get directions ↗
3

Spruce Street Harbor Park

Hammocks, lights and floating barges on the Delaware

SeasonalPenn's LandingFree
Spruce Street Harbor Park

From spring through fall, this pop-up waterfront destination transforms Penn's Landing into a festive oasis of tree-slung hammocks, string lights and floating barge lounges over the Delaware River. Visitors graze on food from local vendors and sip craft beer along the boardwalk while lawn games and colorful nets keep the vibe playful.

Address: 301 S. Columbus Boulevard, Philadelphia, PA 19106
Visit website ↗Get directions ↗
4

Rittenhouse Square

Philly's most elegant urban square

City ParkRittenhouse, Center CityFree
Rittenhouse Square

One of William Penn's five original squares, this leafy Center City park is a genteel gathering place with tree-lined walkways, sculptures, a reflecting pool and a central fountain. Year-round it draws readers, dog walkers, picnickers and artists, plus rotating farmers markets and craft fairs.

Address: 210 W. Rittenhouse Square, Philadelphia, PA 19103
Visit website ↗Get directions ↗
5

Franklin Square

Family square with carousel and mini-golf

City ParkOld CityFree
Franklin Square

This family-friendly Old City square packs acres of green space around one of the country's oldest public fountains, along with a carousel, playgrounds and a Philly-themed mini-golf course. Seasonal daytime and evening water-and-light shows make its historic fountain a nighttime draw.

Address: 200 N. 6th Street, Philadelphia, PA 19106

6

Bartram's Garden

America's oldest surviving botanic garden

GardenSouthwest PhiladelphiaFree
Bartram's Garden

Set on 50 riverfront acres, this National Historic Landmark is America's oldest surviving botanic garden, founded by 18th-century botanist John Bartram and free to the public. Visitors wander fragrant historic gardens, a reclaimed meadow and an urban farm, or head to the public dock for fishing, boating and river views.

Address: 5400 Lindbergh Boulevard, Philadelphia, PA 19143
Visit website ↗Get directions ↗
7

Race Street Pier

Two-tiered pier beneath the Ben Franklin Bridge

RiverfrontDelaware River WaterfrontFree
Race Street Pier

Jutting into the Delaware in the shadow of the Benjamin Franklin Bridge, this two-level pier offers tiered seating, a grassy lawn and sweeping water views. Shade trees and benches make it a favorite spot for picnics, sunsets and a breezy riverside stroll.

Address: Race Street & N. Columbus Boulevard, Philadelphia, PA 19106
Visit website ↗Get directions ↗
8

Schuylkill River Trail

Riverside walking and biking with a scenic boardwalk

TrailCenter City to Fairmount ParkFree
Schuylkill River Trail

This popular multi-use trail runs for miles along the Schuylkill River, linking Center City to the Philadelphia Museum of Art, Fairmount Park and Kelly Drive with a standout over-water boardwalk section. Open year-round, it's a go-to for walking, jogging and cycling, with summer movie nights, kayak tours and scenic river cruises.

Address: 2501 Walnut Street, Philadelphia, PA 19103
Visit website ↗Get directions ↗
9

Washington Square

Historic square and Revolutionary War memorial

City ParkSociety HillFree
Washington Square

Another of Penn's original five squares, this tree-shaded green near Independence Hall carries layered history as a former burial ground and colonial-era gathering place. Today it's a tranquil retreat anchored by the Tomb of the Unknown Revolutionary War Soldier and its eternal flame.

Address: 210 W. Washington Square, Philadelphia, PA 19106
Visit website ↗Get directions ↗
10

FDR Park

South Philly lakes, birding and Southeast Asian Market

City ParkSouth PhiladelphiaFree
FDR Park

Tucked among South Philly's neighborhoods and stadiums, this park spreads across fields, trails, a lagoon and lakes that make it a bird-watcher's haven and a hub for tennis, rugby and baseball. Seasonal highlights include kayak tours, a volunteer-built skate park and the beloved Southeast Asian Market.

Address: 1500 Pattison Avenue, Philadelphia, PA 19145
