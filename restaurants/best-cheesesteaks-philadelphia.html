The 10 Best Cheesesteaks in Philadelphia

Whiz or provolone, wit or witout — here's where to get the city's greatest cheesesteak, from old-school legends to modern upstarts.

PFBy the PhillyFinder team · Updated July 2026

No sandwich is more Philadelphia than the cheesesteak — thin-sliced beef griddled with onions, piled onto a long roll, and blanketed in Cheez Whiz or melted provolone. Everyone in this city has an opinion, and the "best" one depends on whether you're after tourist spectacle, connoisseur craft, or a late-night classic.

We ate our way across the city to rank the ten that matter most. A quick primer on ordering like a local: "whiz wit" means Cheez Whiz with onions; "provolone witout" swaps the cheese and skips them. Now, the list.

1

John's Roast Pork

CheesesteaksPennsport$$
John's Roast PorkPhoto

This James Beard-honored corner shop is widely considered the connoisseur's cheesesteak, built on a crusty seeded roll with sharp provolone that stands up to the juicy, well-seasoned beef. Cash-only and open only during the day, it draws lines of purists who prize balance and quality over hype.

Signature: Cheesesteak with sharp provolone on a seeded roll
14 East Snyder Avenue, Philadelphia, PA 19148
2

Angelo's Pizzeria

CheesesteaksBella Vista$$
Angelo's PizzeriaPhoto

A perpetually packed South Philly pizzeria whose house-baked bread earns it a spot atop many cheesesteak rankings. Expect long waits and a cash-only counter turning out some of the best-constructed steaks in the city, with a chew and flavor that keep the crowds coming back.

Signature: Cheesesteak on a house-baked seeded roll
736 South 9th Street, Philadelphia, PA 19147
3

Pat's King of Steaks

CheesesteaksPassyunk Square$
Pat's King of SteaksPhoto

The self-proclaimed birthplace of the cheesesteak, open around the clock at its famous South Philly corner. It's as much a tourist landmark and late-night ritual as a meal, facing off with Geno's across the street in the city's most iconic sandwich rivalry.

Signature: Classic cheesesteak, "whiz wit"
1237 East Passyunk Avenue, Philadelphia, PA 19147

4

Geno's Steaks

CheesesteaksPassyunk Square$
Geno's SteaksPhoto

The neon-drenched rival that has anchored the opposite corner from Pat's for decades. Its brightly lit 24-hour stand is a bucket-list stop for visitors chasing the classic Philly experience, and the friendly Pat's-vs-Geno's debate is half the fun of the trip.

Signature: Cheesesteak with provolone
1219 South 9th Street, Philadelphia, PA 19147
5

Tony Luke's

CheesesteaksSouth Philadelphia$$
Tony Luke'sPhoto

A South Philly legend near the stadiums, famous for both its cheesesteaks and its iconic roast pork Italian with broccoli rabe and sharp provolone. The bustling walk-up counter is a game-day and late-night institution that locals defend fiercely.

Signature: Cheesesteak — and the roast pork Italian
39 East Oregon Avenue, Philadelphia, PA 19148
6

Dalessandro's Steaks

CheesesteaksRoxborough$$
Dalessandro's SteaksPhoto

A Roxborough institution known for finely chopped, juicy meat piled high on a soft roll. Its classic whiz-and-onions steak is a benchmark for locals across the city's northwest, and the cozy neighborhood setting beats any tourist line.

Signature: Cheesesteak, "whiz wit"
600 Wendover Street, Philadelphia, PA 19128
AdvertisementResponsive · Monetag native / display zone
7

Woodrow's Sandwich Shop

CheesesteaksBella Vista$$
Woodrow's Sandwich ShopPhoto

A modern take on the form, using shaved rib eye and a house-made truffled cheese whiz that has become a cult favorite. The upscale twist makes it the go-to for anyone wanting something a step beyond the traditional sandwich.

Signature: Cheesesteak with house truffled Whiz
630 South Street, Philadelphia, PA 19147
8

Joe's Steaks + Soda Shop

CheesesteaksFishtown$
Joe's Steaks + Soda ShopPhoto

A retro shop turning out griddled steaks from a recipe that's barely changed in more than 60 years. The old-school soda-fountain milkshakes make it a fun, family-friendly Fishtown stop that pairs nostalgia with a genuinely great sandwich.

Signature: Cheesesteak with a milkshake
1 West Girard Avenue, Philadelphia, PA 19123
9

Jim's South St.

CheesesteaksSouth Street$$
Jim's South St.Photo

A South Street icon that reopened after a major fire and is once again griddling its beloved steaks in a rebuilt space. The aroma pouring onto the sidewalk still pulls in a steady line of locals and tourists at all hours.

Signature: Cheesesteak, "whiz wit"
400 South Street, Philadelphia, PA 19147
10

Cleavers

CheesesteaksCenter City$$
CleaversPhoto

A sleek Center City counter that has become a modern go-to, favoring quality beef and gooey Cooper Sharp cheese. Its polished, quick-service format suits the downtown lunch crowd and proves a great steak doesn't require a trip to South Philly.

Signature: Cooper Sharp cheesesteak
108 South 18th Street, Philadelphia, PA 19103
