The 10 Best Cheesesteaks in Philadelphia

Whiz or provolone, wit or witout — here's where to get the city's greatest cheesesteak, from old-school legends to modern upstarts.

GPBy the GuidePhilly team · Updated July 2026
Advertisement728 × 90 · Monetag zone

No sandwich is more Philadelphia than the cheesesteak: thin-sliced beef griddled with onions, piled onto a long roll, and blanketed in Cheez Whiz or melted provolone. Everyone here has an opinion, and the best one depends on whether you're after tourist spectacle, connoisseur craft, or a late-night classic.

A quick primer on ordering like a local: 'whiz wit' means Cheez Whiz with onions; 'provolone witout' swaps the cheese and skips them. Now, the ten that matter most.

1

John's Roast Pork

CheesesteaksPennsport$$
John's Roast Pork

This James Beard-honored corner shop is widely considered the connoisseur's cheesesteak, built on a crusty seeded roll with sharp provolone. Cash-only and daytime-only, it draws lines of purists who prize its balance over hype.

Signature  Cheesesteak with sharp provolone on a seeded roll
Address: 14 East Snyder Avenue, Philadelphia, PA 19148

2

Angelo's Pizzeria

CheesesteaksBella Vista$$
Angelo's Pizzeria

A perpetually packed South Philly pizzeria whose house-baked bread earns it a spot atop many cheesesteak rankings. Expect long waits and a cash-only counter turning out some of the best-constructed steaks in the city.

Signature  Cheesesteak on house-baked seeded roll
Address: 736 South 9th Street, Philadelphia, PA 19147

3

Pat's King of Steaks

CheesesteaksPassyunk Square$
Pat's King of Steaks

The self-proclaimed birthplace of the cheesesteak, open around the clock at its famous South Philly corner. It's as much a tourist landmark and late-night ritual as a meal, facing off with Geno's across the street.

Signature  Classic cheesesteak 'whiz wit'
Address: 1237 East Passyunk Avenue, Philadelphia, PA 19147

4

Geno's Steaks

CheesesteaksPassyunk Square$
Geno's Steaks

The neon-drenched rival that has anchored the opposite corner from Pat's for decades. Its brightly lit 24-hour stand is a bucket-list stop for visitors chasing the classic Philly experience.

Signature  Cheesesteak with provolone
Address: 1219 South 9th Street, Philadelphia, PA 19147

5

Tony Luke's

CheesesteaksSouth Philadelphia$$
Tony Luke's

A South Philly legend near the stadiums, famous for both its cheesesteaks and its iconic roast pork Italian with broccoli rabe and sharp provolone. The bustling walk-up counter is a game-day and late-night institution.

Signature  Cheesesteak and the roast pork Italian
Address: 39 East Oregon Avenue, Philadelphia, PA 19148

Dalessandro's Steaks

CheesesteaksRoxborough$$
Dalessandro's Steaks

A Roxborough institution known for finely chopped, juicy meat piled high on a soft roll. Its classic whiz-and-onions steak is a benchmark for locals across the city's northwest.

Woodrow's Sandwich Shop

CheesesteaksBella Vista$$
Woodrow's Sandwich Shop

A modern take on the form, using shaved rib eye and a house-made truffled cheese whiz. The upscale twist has made it a favorite for those wanting something beyond the traditional sandwich.

Signature  Cheesesteak with house truffled Whiz

Joe's Steaks + Soda Shop

Joe's Steaks + Soda Shop

A retro shop turning out griddled steaks from a recipe that's barely changed in 60 years. The old-school soda-fountain milkshakes make it a fun, family-friendly Fishtown stop.

Jim's South St.

CheesesteaksSouth Street$$
Jim's South St.

A South Street icon that reopened after a major fire and is once again griddling its beloved steaks in a rebuilt space. The aroma pouring onto the sidewalk still pulls in a steady line of locals and tourists.

Cleavers

Cleavers

A sleek Center City counter that has become a modern go-to, favoring quality beef and gooey Cooper Sharp cheese. Its polished, quick-service format suits the downtown lunch crowd.
