The 25 Best Restaurants in Philadelphia

From James Beard winners and Michelin stars to tiny BYOs, these are the tables defining Philadelphia dining right now.

GPBy the GuidePhilly team · Updated July 2026

Philadelphia's dining scene has never been better. Over the past few years the city has racked up James Beard Awards, a growing list of Michelin stars, and a wave of ambitious BYOs that punch far above their size.

We pulled together the restaurants worth building a night, or a whole trip, around. From Southern Thai fireworks to fine-dining Italian and hidden sushi counters, here are the 25 best restaurants in Philadelphia right now.

1

Kalaya

ThaiFishtown$$$
Kalaya

Chef Nok Suntaranon's Southern Thai powerhouse earned a James Beard Outstanding Restaurant award for its bold, layered curries and seafood. The lively Fishtown dining room feels celebratory, with ornate dishes and a depth of Thai flavor rarely seen at this level.

Signature  Tom yum with giant river prawns
Address: 4 West Palmer Street, Philadelphia, PA 19125

2

Zahav

IsraeliSociety Hill$$$
Zahav

Michael Solomonov's landmark Israeli restaurant remains a national reference point for modern Middle Eastern cooking. Silky hummus, wood-fire grilling, and gracious service anchor a meal worth planning a trip around.

Signature  Pomegranate-braised lamb shoulder
Address: 237 Saint James Place, Philadelphia, PA 19106

3

Vetri Cucina

ItalianMidtown Village$$$$
Vetri Cucina

Marc Vetri's long-running temple to Italian fine dining still delivers a flawless, detail-obsessed tasting experience decades in. Impeccable pastas and knowing service keep it among Philadelphia's brightest culinary landmarks.

Signature  Spinach gnocchi
Address: 1312 Spruce Street, Philadelphia, PA 19107

New AmericanRittenhouse$$$$
Friday Saturday Sunday

Chad and Hanna Williams' townhouse restaurant earned a Michelin star for its technical, seasonal, and personal contemporary cooking. Warm service and inventive desserts round out one of the city's most refined nights out.

Address: 261 South 21st Street, Philadelphia, PA 19103

5

Mawn

CambodianBella Vista$$$
Mawn

This tiny 32-seat BYO from Phila and Rachel Lorn serves modern Cambodian cooking with the energy of a nightly celebration. Cold night-market noodles and chili-laced soups have made it one of the most award-decorated tables in the city.

Signature  Wagyu beef noodle soup
Address: 764 South 9th Street, Philadelphia, PA 19147
Visit website ↗Get directions ↗
6

Royal Sushi & Izakaya

JapaneseQueen Village$$$$
Royal Sushi & Izakaya

Behind a bustling izakaya sits a hidden sushi counter serving one of the city's finest omakase experiences. The front room's flavorful small plates and drinks make it a night that works on two levels.

Signature  Hidden-counter omakase

7

Vernick Fish

SeafoodCenter City$$$$
Vernick Fish

Greg Vernick's polished seafood restaurant delivers pristine fish and raw-bar classics in a sleek Comcast-tower setting. Precise cooking and a strong oyster program make it a top-tier special-occasion choice.

Signature  Raw bar and oysters

Laser Wolf

This shipudiya from the Zahav team builds a whole meal around live-fire skewers and an endless spread of salads and hummus. The bright room and set format make it a reliably fun group night out.

9

My Loup

Modern FrenchRittenhouse$$$

Amanda Shulman and Alex Kemp run this lively bistro just off Rittenhouse Square, serving a rotating menu of modern French cooking with a strong raw bar. The room hums with an intimate, celebratory energy that has made it one of the city's toughest reservations.

Signature  Raw bar plateau

Chef Amanda Shulman's intimate supper club serves a warm, communal set menu that feels like dinner at a very talented friend's home. Its personal, seasonal cooking has made reservations among the hardest to land in the city.

James Beard winner Greg Vernick runs this bi-level Rittenhouse brownstone where simply roasted vegetables and fish share the spotlight with his famous toasts. The cooking is refined yet unfussy, cementing its status as a longtime Philadelphia benchmark.

MediterraneanFishtown$$$
Bastia

Tyler Akin's coastal Mediterranean spot channels Corsica and the islands with wood-fired seafood and pastas. Bright flavors and an easy, sun-washed vibe make it one of Fishtown's marquee arrivals.

This plush Stephen Starr steakhouse pairs globally sourced prime cuts and caviar service with a clubby, jewel-box dining room. Its over-the-top Wagyu cheesesteak has become a Philadelphia luxury-dining rite of passage.

Joe Beddia's pizzeria, once dubbed home of the best pizza in America, serves meticulously crafted pies in a bright, plant-filled room. A patio and natural-wine list round out the low-key but destination-worthy experience.

Modern ItalianQueen Village$$$$

Chris D'Ambro and Marina De Oliveira run this itsy-bitsy Queen Village destination offering a modern Italian tasting menu. The tiny footprint and ambitious, personal cooking make it one of the city's most distinctive fine-dining seats.

Amá

MexicanFishtown$$$

Chef Frankie Ramirez cooks a live-fire menu of contemporary Mexican dishes at this buzzy spot on the Fishtown-Kensington border. Smoke-kissed proteins and vibrant tostadas anchor a menu that has landed it among the city's essential new tables.
