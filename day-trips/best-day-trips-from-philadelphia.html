The Best Day Trips from Philadelphia

Beaches, gardens, mountains and Amish country — all within about two hours of the city.

GPBy the GuidePhilly team · Updated July 2026

One of the underrated perks of living in or visiting Philadelphia is how much sits within an easy drive: the Jersey Shore, world-class gardens, historic towns and mountain trails are all day-trip distance.

Here are the best places to escape for the day, no overnight bag required.

1

New Hope

Bucks County arts, antiques and canal strolls

River Town~1 hr northeast$
New Hope

This walkable town on the Delaware River packs art galleries, indie boutiques and riverside restaurants into a few charming blocks. Cross the bridge to Lambertville, ride the New Hope Railroad or wander the towpath for an easy, low-key escape from the city.

Address: New Hope, PA

2

Longwood Gardens

One of the world's great display gardens

Gardens~45 min southwest$$
Longwood Gardens

The crown jewel of the Brandywine Valley, Longwood spreads more than 1,000 acres of manicured grounds, fountains and a vast heated conservatory. Pair it with a nearby winery or the DuPont estates for a full day of gardens and countryside.

Address: Kennett Square, PA
Visit website ↗Get directions ↗
3

Lancaster / Amish Country

Pennsylvania Dutch farms and buggy rides

Historic~1.5 hr west$
Lancaster / Amish Country

Rolling farmland dotted with horse-drawn buggies gives this region its timeless, unhurried feel. Tour a working Amish homestead, browse Lancaster's Central Market and load up on shoofly pie, whoopie pies and roadside farm stands.

Address: Lancaster, PA
Visit website ↗Get directions ↗
4

Cape May

Victorian seaside charm and wide beaches

Beach Town~1.5-2 hr south$
Cape May

America's oldest seaside resort is famous for its candy-colored Victorian houses and gaslit streets. Beyond the sand, you can climb the lighthouse, go birding at the point or sip your way through the town's growing wine and restaurant scene.

Address: Cape May, NJ
Visit website ↗Get directions ↗
5

Atlantic City

Iconic boardwalk, casinos and the shore

Boardwalk~1 hr southeast$
Atlantic City

The six-mile boardwalk fronts a wide beach lined with casinos, arcades and saltwater taffy shops. It's an easy shore fix for a day of gaming, people-watching and ocean views without a long drive.

Address: Atlantic City, NJ
Visit website ↗Get directions ↗
6

Valley Forge

Washington's Revolutionary War encampment

Historic~40 min northwestFree
Valley Forge

This sprawling national park preserves the ground where Washington's Continental Army endured the winter of 1777-78. Miles of trails, reconstructed log huts and a visitor center make it as good for a bike ride or picnic as for a history lesson.

Address: King of Prussia, PA
Visit website ↗Get directions ↗
7

Sesame Place

Sesame Street theme and water park

Family~40 min northeast$$
Sesame Place

The only U.S. theme park built entirely around Sesame Street, it blends gentle rides, character parades and a full water park for young kids. It's tailor-made for families with preschoolers looking for a manageable, one-day outing.

Address: Langhorne, PA
Visit website ↗Get directions ↗
8

Princeton

Ivy League campus and Grounds For Sculpture

College Town~1 hr northeastFree
Princeton

Stroll the Gothic quads of Princeton University, then browse the shops and cafes around Palmer Square and Nassau Street. Nearby Grounds For Sculpture adds 42 acres of gardens studded with playful, large-scale artworks.

Address: Princeton, NJ
Visit website ↗Get directions ↗
9

The Poconos

Mountain waterfalls, rivers and resorts

Nature~1.5-2 hr north$
The Poconos

This forested mountain region offers waterfall hikes, whitewater on the Lehigh and scenic rail rides through the gorge. In warmer months it's all about lakes and trails, while historic Jim Thorpe makes a picturesque base for wandering.

Address: Pocono Mountains, PA
Visit website ↗Get directions ↗
10

Winterthur

Du Pont estate, decorative arts and gardens

Gardens & Museum~45 min southwest$$
Winterthur

Henry Francis du Pont's former country estate houses one of the finest collections of American decorative arts, set within a romantic 60-acre naturalistic garden. Kids can explore the whimsical Enchanted Woods while adults tour the mansion's period rooms.

Address: Winterthur, DE
Visit website ↗Get directions ↗
11

Hershey

Chocolate-themed town and Hersheypark

Family~1.5 hr west$$
Hershey

Pennsylvania's sweetest town centers on Hersheypark, the state's largest amusement park with dozens of coasters and a summer water park. Cap the day at the free Hershey's Chocolate World tour, where even the streetlights are shaped like Kisses.

Address: Hershey, PA
