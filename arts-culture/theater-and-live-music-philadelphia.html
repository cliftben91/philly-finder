Theater & Live Music in Philadelphia

From the home of the Philadelphia Orchestra to intimate Fishtown clubs, here's where to catch a show in Philly.

GPBy the GuidePhilly team · Updated July 2026

Philadelphia's performing-arts scene runs from grand historic opera houses to sweaty indie clubs, much of it clustered along the Avenue of the Arts and in Fishtown.

Here are the concert halls, theaters and live-music rooms worth building a night out around.

1

Kimmel Center for the Performing Arts

Home of the Philadelphia Orchestra

Concert HallAvenue of the Arts$$$
Kimmel Center for the Performing Arts

This glass-vaulted landmark on Broad Street anchors Philadelphia's Avenue of the Arts and houses the acoustically celebrated 2,500-seat Marian Anderson Hall (formerly Verizon Hall). Beyond orchestral concerts, its stages host jazz, Broadway tours, dance and family programming throughout the year.

Address: 300 S. Broad Street, Philadelphia, PA 19102

2

Academy of Music

The nation's oldest continuously operating opera house

OperaAvenue of the Arts$$$
Academy of Music

Opened in 1857, this opulent, chandelier-lit hall is a National Historic Landmark and the resident home of Opera Philadelphia and the Philadelphia Ballet. Its gilded, horseshoe-shaped auditorium delivers grand productions and touring Broadway shows in a genuinely historic setting.

Address: 240 S. Broad Street, Philadelphia, PA 19102
Visit website ↗Get directions ↗
3

The Met Philadelphia

Restored 1908 opera house turned concert palace

Concert HallNorth Broad Street$$
The Met Philadelphia

Originally built as an opera house in 1908, this North Broad landmark reopened in 2018 after a lavish restoration and now packs its ornate 3,400-capacity hall with major touring artists. It draws big-name pop, rock, R&B and comedy acts to a room that blends grandeur with modern production.

Address: 858 N. Broad Street, Philadelphia, PA 19130
Visit website ↗Get directions ↗
4

The Mann Center

Premier outdoor summer concert amphitheater

AmphitheaterFairmount Park$$
The Mann Center

Set amid the greenery of Fairmount Park with sweeping views of the skyline, the Mann is Philadelphia's signature warm-weather venue for open-air concerts. Its covered pavilion and sprawling lawn host everything from the Philadelphia Orchestra's summer series to marquee touring headliners.

Address: 5201 Parkside Avenue, Philadelphia, PA 19131
Visit website ↗Get directions ↗
5

Franklin Music Hall

Historic 2,500-capacity room, the former Electric Factory

Live MusicCallowhill$$
Franklin Music HallPhoto

This cavernous former factory, long known as the Electric Factory, is one of the city's largest general-admission rooms and a magnet for national touring acts. Standing-room floor space and a balcony bar give it a raw, high-energy concert atmosphere.

Address: 421 N. 7th Street, Philadelphia, PA 19123
Visit website ↗Get directions ↗
6

Union Transfer

One of the city's best-sounding indie rooms

Live MusicCallowhill$$
Union Transfer

Housed in a former train depot, Union Transfer is beloved for its excellent acoustics, sliding stage and multiple bars that scale to any crowd size. It's a go-to stop for indie, rock and rising national artists, consistently ranked among Philadelphia's top mid-sized venues.

Address: 1026 Spring Garden Street, Philadelphia, PA 19123
Visit website ↗Get directions ↗
7

World Cafe Live

Intimate concerts and home of WXPN radio

Live MusicUniversity City$$
World Cafe LivePhoto

Connected to public radio station WXPN, this two-stage venue pairs an upstairs listening room with a larger downstairs hall offering full food and drink service. Its refined, intimate setting makes it a favorite for singer-songwriters, jazz and roots artists.

Address: 3025 Walnut Street, Philadelphia, PA 19104
Visit website ↗Get directions ↗
8

The Fillmore Philadelphia

Converted Fishtown factory with a chandelier-lit hall

Live MusicFishtown$$
The Fillmore PhiladelphiaPhoto

A restored industrial building in trendy Fishtown, the Fillmore hosts rising and established acts in its spacious main hall and the smaller upstairs Foundry room. Its vintage decor, big-name bookings and multiple bars make it a cornerstone of the neighborhood's nightlife.

Address: 29 E. Allen Street, Philadelphia, PA 19123
Visit website ↗Get directions ↗
9

Walnut Street Theatre

America's oldest theatre, founded in 1808

TheaterWashington Square West$$$
Walnut Street Theatre

Founded in 1808, the Walnut is the oldest continuously operating theatre in the English-speaking world and the most subscribed theatre company in the nation. It stages a popular season of Broadway-style musicals, comedies and dramas in its intimate main auditorium.

Address: 825 Walnut Street, Philadelphia, PA 19107
Visit website ↗Get directions ↗
10

Wilma Theater

Bold, contemporary and internationally minded productions

TheaterAvenue of the Arts$$
Wilma Theater

A Tony Award-winning company on the Avenue of the Arts, the Wilma is known for adventurous, boundary-pushing contemporary theater and its resident acting ensemble. Its modern 300-seat space offers an intimate vantage on ambitious new work and reimagined classics.

Address: 265 S. Broad Street, Philadelphia, PA 19107
Visit website ↗Get directions ↗
11

Xfinity Mobile Arena

The city's largest arena, formerly the Wells Fargo Center

ArenaSouth Philadelphia$$
Xfinity Mobile Arena

Renamed the Xfinity Mobile Arena in 2025, this 21,000-seat venue in the South Philadelphia sports complex is home to the 76ers and Flyers. It also hosts the region's biggest arena concerts and touring spectacles year-round.

Address: 3601 S. Broad Street, Philadelphia, PA 19148
Visit website ↗Get directions ↗
12

Johnny Brenda's

Beloved intimate Fishtown club with a horseshoe balcony

Live MusicFishtown$$
Johnny Brenda'sPhoto

A cornerstone of Fishtown's music scene, Johnny Brenda's pairs a well-regarded gastropub downstairs with an intimate upstairs concert room ringed by a horseshoe balcony. Its sharp booking of indie and emerging acts makes it one of the city's most cherished small venues.

Address: 1201 N. Frankford Avenue, Philadelphia, PA 19125
