The Best Museums in Philadelphia

World-class art, hands-on science, medical oddities and founding-era history — Philadelphia is one of America's great museum cities.

PFBy the PhillyFinder team · Updated July 2026
Advertisement728 × 90 · Monetag zone

Few American cities pack as many great museums into as small a footprint as Philadelphia. The Benjamin Franklin Parkway alone strings together several world-class institutions in an easy walk.

From Impressionist masterpieces to a walk-through human heart, here are the museums worth planning a day around.

1

Philadelphia Museum of Art

Home of the Rocky Steps and a world-class collection

Art MuseumBenjamin Franklin Parkway$$
Philadelphia Museum of Art

One of the largest art museums in the country, its 200-plus galleries hold masterworks by Van Gogh, Picasso, Duchamp and Cezanne alongside a Japanese teahouse and suits of armor. The grand Beaux-Arts building crowning the Parkway is as much a draw as the art inside, right down to the famous stone steps out front.

Address: 2600 Benjamin Franklin Pkwy, Philadelphia, PA 19130

2

Barnes Foundation

The world's largest holdings of Renoir and Cezanne

Art MuseumBenjamin Franklin Parkway$$
Barnes Foundation

Chemist Albert Barnes assembled a staggering trove of Impressionist and Modernist paintings and arranged them by color and form rather than chronology, mingling European canvases with African masks and ironwork. The unconventional, densely packed galleries make it one of the most distinctive art experiences in America.

Address: 2025 Benjamin Franklin Pkwy, Philadelphia, PA 19130
Visit website ↗Get directions ↗
3

The Franklin Institute

Hands-on science and the giant walk-through heart

ScienceLogan Square$$
The Franklin Institute

Named for Benjamin Franklin, this sprawling science center rewards curiosity with interactive exhibits on the brain, electricity, space and flight. Kids and adults alike line up to stroll through the two-story walk-through heart and marvel at the towering marble Franklin memorial in the rotunda.

Address: 222 N 20th St, Philadelphia, PA 19103
Visit website ↗Get directions ↗
4

Please Touch Museum

Philly's premier hands-on museum built for young kids

Children'sFairmount Park$$
Please Touch Museum

Designed entirely around play, this children's museum fills the historic Memorial Hall with interactive worlds where little ones can shop a pretend supermarket, pilot a SEPTA bus and splash at water tables. A restored 1908 carousel and constant programming keep families coming back.

Address: 4231 Avenue of the Republic, Philadelphia, PA 19131
Visit website ↗Get directions ↗
5

Eastern State Penitentiary

A crumbling gothic prison that once held Al Capone

HistoryFairmount$$
Eastern State Penitentiary

Once the most famous and expensive prison in the world, this hauntingly beautiful ruin pioneered the idea of solitary confinement and reform. Visitors wander the eerie cellblocks with an atmospheric audio tour, and each fall the site transforms into a notorious haunted attraction.

Address: 2027 Fairmount Ave, Philadelphia, PA 19130

6

Mutter Museum

Medical oddities and anatomical curiosities

SpecialtyCenter City West$$
Mutter Museum

Run by the College of Physicians of Philadelphia, this gloriously macabre museum displays antique medical instruments, preserved organs and curiosities like slices of Einstein's brain and the conjoined liver of Chang and Eng Bunker. It is equal parts fascinating and unsettling, and unlike anything else in the city.

Address: 19 S 22nd St, Philadelphia, PA 19103
Visit website ↗Get directions ↗
7

National Constitution Center

The only museum dedicated to the U.S. Constitution

HistoryOld City$$
National Constitution Center

Just steps from Independence Hall, this interactive museum brings the nation's founding document to life through high-tech exhibits, a stirring live theater production and a hall of life-size bronze statues of the signers. A newly expanded founding gallery makes it a timely stop for the country's 250th anniversary.

Address: 525 Arch St, Philadelphia, PA 19106
Visit website ↗Get directions ↗
8

Rodin Museum

The largest Rodin collection outside Paris

Art MuseumBenjamin Franklin Parkway$
Rodin Museum

This intimate Beaux-Arts jewel box on the Parkway holds bronzes and plasters by Auguste Rodin, the father of modern sculpture, including a cast of The Thinker at its gates. Admission is pay-what-you-wish, making a quiet hour with The Burghers of Calais and The Gates of Hell an easy pleasure.

Address: 2151 Benjamin Franklin Pkwy, Philadelphia, PA 19130
Visit website ↗Get directions ↗
9

Penn Museum

Ancient treasures including a 15-ton Egyptian sphinx

HistoryUniversity City$$
Penn Museum

The University of Pennsylvania's museum of archaeology and anthropology guards more than a million artifacts drawn from Egypt, Mesopotamia, Greece, Asia and beyond. Standouts include the monumental granite sphinx, real mummies and a soaring Chinese rotunda that transports visitors across millennia.

Address: 3260 South St, Philadelphia, PA 19104
Visit website ↗Get directions ↗
10

African American Museum in Philadelphia

The first major U.S. museum built to celebrate Black history

HistoryOld City$
African American Museum in Philadelphia

Founded in 1976, this Old City institution tells the story of African American life, art and achievement through rotating exhibitions and its core 'Audacious Freedom' experience on Philadelphia's early Black community. Its accessible galleries make centuries of history both moving and immediate.

Address: 701 Arch St, Philadelphia, PA 19106
Visit website ↗Get directions ↗

11

Independence Seaport Museum

Maritime history and historic ships on the Delaware

HistoryPenn's Landing$
Independence Seaport Museum

Perched on the Penn's Landing waterfront, this museum explores the region's deep ties to rivers and the sea through interactive galleries and boat-building demonstrations. Admission includes climbing aboard the Cruiser Olympia, the oldest floating steel warship in the world, and the WWII submarine Becuna.

Address: 211 S Columbus Blvd, Philadelphia, PA 19106
Visit website ↗Get directions ↗
12

Museum of the American Revolution

Home to George Washington's original war tent

HistoryOld City$$
Museum of the American Revolution

This immersive Old City museum recounts the full sweep of the Revolution through weapons, art, interactive theaters and thousands of period artifacts. Its centerpiece is the very tent Washington used as his field command, revealed in a dramatic short presentation.

Address: 101 S 3rd St, Philadelphia, PA 19106
