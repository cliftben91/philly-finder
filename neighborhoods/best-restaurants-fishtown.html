The Best Restaurants in Fishtown

Philadelphia's buzziest food neighborhood, from a James Beard-winning Thai flagship to live-fire grills and destination pizza.

GPBy the GuidePhilly team · Updated July 2026

Once a working-class fishing district, Fishtown has become the single most exciting place to eat in Philadelphia. Its blocks of converted factories and rowhomes now hold some of the city's most acclaimed restaurants, side by side with great bars and coffee.

Here are the Fishtown tables worth crossing town for.

1

Kalaya

ThaiFishtown$$$
Kalaya

Nok Suntaranon's award-winning Southern Thai flagship is Fishtown's crown jewel, serving vivid curries and whole grilled fish. The stylish, buzzy room makes it a destination worth booking well ahead.

Signature  Tom yum with giant river prawns
Address: 4 West Palmer Street, Philadelphia, PA 19125

2

Laser Wolf

IsraeliFishtown$$$
Laser Wolf

The Zahav team's live-fire shipudiya builds a full meal around grilled skewers and an endless spread of salads, pita, and hummus. The set-price format and lively vibe make it a great group destination.

Signature  Skewers with bottomless salatim
Address: 1301 North Howard Street, Philadelphia, PA 19122
Visit website ↗Get directions ↗
3

Suraya

LebaneseFishtown$$$
Suraya

A sprawling Lebanese cafe, market, and restaurant with a gorgeous garden patio. Mornings bring pastries and coffee, while dinner delivers mezze and wood-fired kebabs in one of the neighborhood's prettiest spaces.

Signature  Lamb kebabs
Address: 1528 Frankford Avenue, Philadelphia, PA 19125
Visit website ↗Get directions ↗
4

Wm. Mulherin's Sons

ItalianFishtown$$$
Wm. Mulherin's Sons

Set in a restored former whiskey distillery, this rustic-chic restaurant turns out wood-fired pizzas and Italian plates. The handsome, brick-and-timber room also anchors a boutique hotel upstairs.

Signature  Wood-fired seasonal pizzas
Address: 1355 North Front Street, Philadelphia, PA 19122
Visit website ↗Get directions ↗
5

Middle Child Clubhouse

AmericanFishtown$$
Middle Child Clubhouse

The sit-down big sibling of the beloved Middle Child luncheonette, serving inventive sandwiches by day and a whimsical American menu by night. Its playful, retro-clubhouse energy makes it a Fishtown favorite.

Signature  Signature hoagies and sandwiches
Address: 1232 North Front Street, Philadelphia, PA 19122
Visit website ↗Get directions ↗
6

Amá

MexicanFishtown$$$
Amá

An upscale modern Mexican restaurant with sharp regional cooking and a serious cocktail program. Dishes like crisp tostadas and inventive small plates give it a polished, date-night energy.

Signature  Tuna tostadas
Address: 101 West Oxford Street, Philadelphia, PA 19122
Visit website ↗Get directions ↗
7

Bastia

MediterraneanFishtown$$$
Bastia

Tyler Akin's coastal Mediterranean spot channels Corsica with wood-fired seafood and bright, sun-washed pastas. It's one of the most talked-about recent additions to the neighborhood.

Signature  Grilled half lobster algherese
Address: 1401 East Susquehanna Avenue, Philadelphia, PA 19125
Visit website ↗Get directions ↗
8

El Chingon

MexicanFishtown$$
El Chingon

A casual, craveable taqueria known for its in-house baked tortas and sourdough flour tortillas. Bold salsas and a relaxed vibe make it a reliable neighborhood go-to.

Signature  Tortas on house sourdough tortillas
Address: 1431 Frankford Avenue, Philadelphia, PA 19125
Visit website ↗Get directions ↗
9

Pizzeria Beddia

PizzaFishtown$$
Pizzeria Beddia

Joe Beddia's pizzeria, once dubbed home of the best pizza in America, serves meticulously crafted pies in a bright, plant-filled room. A patio and natural-wine list round out the low-key but destination-worthy experience.

Signature  Signature pies and the 'Hoagie'
Address: 1313 North Lee Street, Philadelphia, PA 19125
Visit website ↗Get directions ↗
10

Frankford Hall

Beer GardenFishtown$$
Frankford Hall

Stephen Starr's sprawling beer garden pours giant steins alongside pretzels, sausages, and pub fare. Communal tables, ping-pong, and fire pits make it a go-to for a big, laid-back group hang.

Signature  Bavarian pretzels and liter beers
Address: 1210 Frankford Avenue, Philadelphia, PA 19125
