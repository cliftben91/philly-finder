The Best Restaurants in Manayunk

Main Street's hillside strip of gastropubs, breweries, and easygoing neighborhood spots.

GPBy the GuidePhilly team · Updated July 2026

Manayunk's Main Street hugs the Schuylkill with a walkable strip of gastropubs, a riverside brewery, and easygoing neighborhood restaurants that are perfect after a day on the towpath.

Here are the best restaurants in Manayunk right now.

1

The Goat's Beard

GastropubManayunk$$
The Goat's Beard

This cozy, rustic Main Street mainstay turns locally sourced ingredients into elevated comfort food and shareable bar bites. Regulars come back for the cheesesteak egg rolls, Detroit-style pizza, and a friendly bar that keeps the room buzzing.

Signature  Cheesesteak egg rolls
Address: 4201 Main St, Philadelphia, PA 19127

2

Lucky's Last Chance

BurgersManayunk$$
Lucky's Last Chance

A beloved Main Street burger-and-dog joint dressing up its griddled classics with toppings like mac and cheese, chili, and pulled pork. It doubles as one of the warmest bars in the neighborhood, with a deep lineup of beers and cocktails.

Signature  Loaded hot dogs
Address: 4421 Main St, Philadelphia, PA 19127

Bar Jawn

AmericanManayunk$$
Bar Jawn

From the team behind the acclaimed Pizza Jawn, this spot serves gooey, saucy comfort food with an unmistakably Philly accent. Expect generous portions of chicken parm, cheesesteaks, wings, and burgers in a laid-back setting.

Jake's and Cooper's

Jake's and Cooper's

A Main Street institution for nearly four decades, pairing an extensive wine list with polished American plates and gourmet pizzas. Longtime favorites like the crab cakes, short rib pizza, and ribeye keep the dining room busy.

Signature  Crab cakes

SOMO

A colorful, family-friendly taqueria dishing out approachable Mexican favorites like chicken tinga tacos, street corn, and enchiladas. A lengthy tequila and cocktail list plus daily specials make it a lively happy-hour destination.

Yanako

JapaneseManayunk$$
