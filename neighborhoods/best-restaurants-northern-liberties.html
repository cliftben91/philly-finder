The Best Restaurants in Northern Liberties

A trendy, walkable district of vegan fine dining, brunch spots, and global flavors.

PFBy the PhillyFinder team · Updated July 2026

Once industrial and now one of the city's trendiest neighborhoods, Northern Liberties has become a magnet for ambitious restaurants, from nationally praised vegan cooking to packed brunch spots and global BYOs.

Here are the best restaurants in Northern Liberties right now.

1

Pietramala

VeganNorthern Liberties$$$
PietramalaPhoto

This ambitious plant-based kitchen turns humble vegetables into refined, produce-driven plates that change with the seasons. It has become one of the most celebrated vegan dining rooms in Philadelphia and lands high on citywide best-of lists.

Signature  Roasted comb tooth mushrooms
Address: 614 N 2nd St, Philadelphia, PA 19123
2

Cafe La Maude

French-LebaneseNorthern Liberties$$
Cafe La MaudePhoto

A perpetually packed brunch destination that blends French technique with Lebanese flavors across an all-day menu. Weekend waits are common, driven by standouts like the ricotta pancakes and brisket huevos rancheros.

Signature  Lemon ricotta pancakes
Address: 816 N 4th St, Philadelphia, PA 19123
3

Apricot Stone

ArmenianNorthern Liberties$$
Apricot StonePhoto

This intimate BYOB serves warm, spice-forward Armenian and Mediterranean cooking in a cozy setting well suited to date nights. Hummus plates, boreg, and char-grilled kebabs anchor the menu.

Signature  Lamb kebabs
Address: 1040 N American St, Philadelphia, PA 19123
4

Anejo

MexicanNorthern Liberties$$
AnejoPhoto

A lively Mexican spot known for a wide-ranging taco lineup and a deep tequila and mezcal program. The bar stocks both familiar bottles and harder-to-find agave spirits.

Signature  Tacos & mezcal
Address: 1001 N 2nd St, Philadelphia, PA 19123
5

Pera Turkish Cuisine

TurkishNorthern Liberties$$
Pera Turkish CuisinePhoto

One of the few dedicated Turkish tables in the city, this BYOB leans confidently into bold Anatolian spices and sauces. The kebabs and grilled seafood are the dishes to build a meal around.

Signature  Grilled kebabs
Address: 944 N 2nd St, Philadelphia, PA 19123

6

Heritage

New AmericanNorthern Liberties$$$
HeritagePhoto

Part restaurant and part live-music lounge, Heritage pairs an inventive New American menu with generous cocktails and a rotating roster of local performers. The atmosphere makes it as much a night out as a dinner.

Signature  Bistro steak
Address: 914 N 2nd St, Philadelphia, PA 19123
7

Yanaga Kappo Izakaya

JapaneseNorthern Liberties$$$$
Yanaga Kappo IzakayaPhoto

Chef Kevin Yanaga's izakaya delivers precise Japanese small plates alongside a hidden speakeasy-style omakase counter. The multi-course tasting is among the neighborhood's most sought-after seats.

Signature  Omakase counter
Address: 637 N 3rd St, Philadelphia, PA 19123
8

Mana Modern Chinese

ChineseNorthern Liberties$$
Mana Modern ChinesePhoto

A newer arrival from restaurateur Tom Lau, Mana turns out polished modern Chinese cooking in a stylish room. Six varieties of soup dumplings share the menu with classics like kung pao chicken.

Signature  Soup dumplings
Address: 719 N 2nd St, Philadelphia, PA 19123
9

Terra Grill

SteakhouseNorthern Liberties$$$$
Terra GrillPhoto

Michelin-pedigree chef Laurent Tourondel's fire-driven steakhouse opened in 2026 with globally inspired plates spanning Italy, Asia, and the Mediterranean. Expect upscale surprises alongside prime cuts and wood-fired mains.

Signature  Wood-fired branzino
Address: 1099 Germantown Ave, Philadelphia, PA 19123
10

Silk City

American DinerNorthern Liberties$$
Silk CityPhoto

A longtime neighborhood institution combining a classic diner with an attached bar and music venue. It serves comforting all-day fare from filet and eggs to burgers while doubling as a live-performance hangout.

Signature  Loaded pancakes
Address: 435 Spring Garden St, Philadelphia, PA 19123
