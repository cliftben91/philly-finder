The Best Restaurants in East Passyunk

South Philly's foodie playground: a restaurant row of Filipino, Italian, and inventive BYOs.

GPBy the GuidePhilly team · Updated July 2026
Advertisement728 × 90 · Monetag zone

Few streets in Philadelphia pack more great restaurants per block than East Passyunk Avenue, a South Philly strip that runs from century-old Italian to award-winning Filipino and Vietnamese.

Here are the best restaurants in East Passyunk right now.

1

Perla

FilipinoEast Passyunk$$$
Perla

Chef Lou Boquila turns the flavors of the Philippines into a refined, celebratory dining experience just off the Avenue. Rich, garlicky roast pork and bright, vinegary sides anchor a menu that helped put modern Filipino cooking on Philly's map.

Signature  Lechon (crispy whole roast pork)
Address: 1535 S 11th St, Philadelphia, PA 19147

2

The Dutch

AmericanEast Passyunk$$
The Dutch

This sunny corner spot has built a devoted following for its all-day brunch and comfort-driven American plates. Weekends bring long waits for pillowy French toast and hearty egg dishes served in a cheerful, casual room.

Signature  Challah French toast
Address: 1537 S 11th St, Philadelphia, PA 19147

3

River Twice

New AmericanEast Passyunk$$$$
River Twice

Chef Randy Rucker runs an intimate, ever-changing tasting menu built around hyper-seasonal local ingredients and live-fire technique. The result is one of the neighborhood's most ambitious and creative fine-dining destinations.

Signature  Chef's tasting menu
Address: 1601 E Passyunk Ave, Philadelphia, PA 19148

4

Townsend

FrenchEast Passyunk$$$$
Townsend

Chef Townsend Wentz delivers polished, classic French cooking in an elegant white-tablecloth setting on the Avenue. It remains a go-to for special occasions and one of South Philly's most accomplished upscale kitchens.

Address: 1623 E Passyunk Ave, Philadelphia, PA 19148

5

Mr. Martino's Trattoria

ItalianEast Passyunk$$
Mr. Martino's Trattoria

A charming, old-school South Philly trattoria open only a few nights a week, serving homemade pasta in a nostalgic, dimly lit room. The BYOB format and vintage feel make it a beloved throwback among locals.

Signature  Homemade pasta
Address: 1646 E Passyunk Ave, Philadelphia, PA 19148

Pizzata Pizzeria

PizzaEast Passyunk$$
Pizzata Pizzeria

This lively pizzeria fires up authentic Neapolitan-style pies with blistered, airy crusts and pairs them with a deep Italian beer list. It's a relaxed, family-friendly stop for wood-fired pizza on the Avenue.

7

Gabriella's Vietnam

VietnameseEast Passyunk$$$
Gabriella's Vietnam

This James Beard-recognized spot elevates Vietnamese cooking with dishes like delicate open-faced dumplings and whole fish steamed in a gourd. Warm hospitality and precise, vibrant flavors make it a standout on the strip.

Signature  Gourd-steamed branzino

8

Stargazy

BritishEast Passyunk$$
Stargazy

Chef Sam Jacobson brings a taste of Britain to South Philly with hand-raised pork pies, Scotch eggs, and a hearty Sunday roast. The cozy shop is a one-of-a-kind destination for savory British classics.

Signature  British pork pies

Le Virtu

ItalianEast Passyunk$$$
Le Virtu

This destination for the rustic cuisine of Italy's Abruzzo region turns out hand-cut pastas and fire-grilled lamb skewers with serious authenticity. A lovely garden patio and deep regional wine list round out the experience.

A tiny, wildly popular BYOB known for its house-made sourdough tortillas and bold, from-scratch Mexican cooking. Standout tacos and the overstuffed cemita sandwich keep the line out the door.
