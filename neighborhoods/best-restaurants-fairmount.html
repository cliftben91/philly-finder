The Best Restaurants in Fairmount

The Art Museum area's mix of Italian standouts, cozy BYOs, and neighborhood gems.

PFBy the PhillyFinder team · Updated July 2026

Just behind the Art Museum, Fairmount pairs a laid-back residential feel with a surprisingly deep dining bench, from destination Italian to cozy BYOs and reliable neighborhood taverns.

Here are the best restaurants in Fairmount right now.

1

Osteria

ItalianFairmount$$$
OsteriaPhoto

Chef Jeff Michaud channels seasonal northern Italian cooking into wood-fired dishes and handmade pastas at this longtime North Broad standout. A 300-bottle Italian wine cellar and daily happy hour make it as suited to a splurge as to a casual glass at the bar.

Signature  Honeynut squash caramelle
Address: 640 North Broad Street, Philadelphia, PA 19130
2

A Mano

ItalianFairmount$$$
A ManoPhoto

Townsend Wentz's intimate BYOB builds its prix-fixe menus around handmade pasta and family-style comfort classics. It is a reliable choice for a warm, unhurried dinner with a group, where each person picks a dish per course.

Signature  Ricotta cavatelli
Address: 2244 Fairmount Avenue, Philadelphia, PA 19130
3

Umai Umai

JapaneseFairmount$$$
Umai UmaiPhoto

This cozy BYOB sushi spot turns out inventive specialty rolls alongside fresh nigiri and Japanese small plates. Its creative, flavor-forward approach has made it a Fairmount favorite for date nights and special occasions.

Signature  Eternal Sunshine roll
Address: 533 North 22nd Street, Philadelphia, PA 19130
4

Zorba's Tavern

GreekFairmount$$
Zorba's TavernPhoto

A decades-old neighborhood institution serving traditional Greek plates from gyros and hummus to shared roasts. The white-tablecloth room works equally well for a casual midday meal or a relaxed dinner over a bottle of red.

Signature  Roasted rack of lamb
Address: 2230 Fairmount Avenue, Philadelphia, PA 19130
5

Bad Brother

New AmericanFairmount$$
Bad BrotherPhoto

An ambitious gastropub where regulars gather over sausage rolls, house cheeseburgers, and a rotation of nightly specials. The wide-ranging drink list spans cider, mead, wine, and cocktails, including non-alcoholic options.

Signature  Gochujang chicken wings
Address: 726 North 24th Street, Philadelphia, PA 19130

6

Jack's Firehouse

AmericanFairmount$$$
Jack's FirehousePhoto

A farm-to-table mainstay of more than 35 years, serving scratch-made American fare across brunch, lunch, and dinner. Steaks, fish, and St. Louis-cut ribs anchor a menu paired with a pleasant open-air bar.

Signature  Crab cakes
Address: 2130 Fairmount Avenue, Philadelphia, PA 19130
7

Bar Hygge

GastropubFairmount$$
Bar HyggePhoto

Named for the Danish idea of coziness, this warm brewpub pours house-brewed beers in a living-room-like setting. The menu leans on elevated bar classics and customizable snacking boards meant for lingering.

Signature  House-brewed beer
Address: 1720 Fairmount Avenue, Philadelphia, PA 19130
8

South Jazz Kitchen

SouthernFairmount$$$
South Jazz KitchenPhoto

A supper club pairing well-executed Southern cooking with live jazz performances on North Broad. Ticketed shows and classic cocktails like the Sazerac make it a destination for dinner and music together.

Signature  Dinner & live jazz
Address: 600 North Broad Street, Philadelphia, PA 19130
9

La Calaca Feliz

MexicanFairmount$$
La Calaca FelizPhoto

A bright, festive spot bringing Mexican flavors and an extensive tequila list to Fairmount Avenue. Fish tacos and tortilla soup headline a menu that fills a gap in the neighborhood's dining scene.

Signature  Fish tacos
Address: 2321 Fairmount Avenue, Philadelphia, PA 19130
10

Figs

MediterraneanFairmount$$
FigsPhoto

A cash-only, BYOB neighborhood favorite with a Mediterranean menu shaped by Moroccan influences. Longtime regulars prize its brunch, where Moroccan eggs in spicy tomato sauce share the table with buttermilk pancakes.

Signature  Lamb shank tagine
Address: 2501 Meredith Street, Philadelphia, PA 19130
