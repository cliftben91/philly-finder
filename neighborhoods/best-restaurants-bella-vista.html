The Best Restaurants in Bella Vista

The Italian Market's home turf of century-old red-sauce icons and modern Mexican.

GPBy the GuidePhilly team · Updated July 2026
Advertisement728 × 90 · Monetag zone

Home to the 9th Street Italian Market, Bella Vista is where Philadelphia's red-sauce heritage lives on, alongside a new wave of acclaimed Mexican kitchens and a Vetri pasta bar.

Here are the best restaurants in Bella Vista right now.

1

Fiorella

ItalianBella Vista$$$
Fiorella

Marc Vetri's tiny pasta bar tucked into a former butcher shop turns out a short, ever-changing menu of fresh handmade pastas. Seats are scarce and the vibe is intimate, making it one of the neighborhood's most coveted reservations.

Signature  Ricotta gnocchi
Address: 817 Christian Street, Philadelphia, PA 19147

2

Ralph's Italian Restaurant

ItalianBella Vista$$
Ralph's Italian Restaurant

Open since 1900, Ralph's is a red-sauce institution that has fed everyone from Frank Sinatra to sitting presidents. Generous plates of old-school Italian-American classics come with the warm, time-capsule dining room that defines the block.

Signature  Veal parmigiana
Address: 760 S. 9th Street, Philadelphia, PA 19147
Visit website ↗Get directions ↗
3

Dante & Luigi's

ItalianBella Vista$$$
Dante & Luigi's

One of the country's oldest continuously operating Italian restaurants, Dante & Luigi's has anchored the corner of 10th and Catharine since 1899. Career waiters and a menu of handmade pastas and hearty gravy dishes keep the old-world tradition intact.

Signature  Chicken parmigiana
Address: 762 S. 10th Street, Philadelphia, PA 19147
Visit website ↗Get directions ↗
4

Villa di Roma

ItalianBella Vista$$
Villa di Roma

Right in the heart of the 9th Street market, this family-run holdover serves the kind of no-frills South Philly red-sauce cooking that locals grew up on. Expect big portions, a bustling bar, and an unpretentious feel.

Signature  Sunday gravy with meatballs
Address: 936 S. 9th Street, Philadelphia, PA 19147
Visit website ↗Get directions ↗
5

Saloon

Italian SteakhouseBella Vista$$$$
Saloon

A clubby, dimly lit destination for special occasions, Saloon pairs prime steaks and chops with a deep Italian menu and a serious wine list. It has been one of the neighborhood's splurge spots for decades.

Signature  Veal chop
Address: 750 S. 7th Street, Philadelphia, PA 19147
Visit website ↗Get directions ↗

6

Mawn

CambodianBella Vista$$$
Mawn

This acclaimed BYOB noodle house channels Cambodian and broader Southeast Asian flavors into a tight, produce-driven menu. The crab-fat noodles, roti canai, and whole fish have made it one of the hardest tables to land in South Philly.

Signature  Wagyu beef noodle soup
Address: 764 S. 9th Street, Philadelphia, PA 19147
Visit website ↗Get directions ↗
7

Casa Mexico

MexicanBella Vista$$
Casa Mexico

From James Beard-winning chef Cristina Martinez, Casa Mexico delivers deeply flavored, regionally authentic Mexican cooking a few doors from her legendary South Philly Barbacoa. The citrus-and-chile marinated pibil pork is a standout.

Signature  Pibil pork tacos
Address: 1134 S. 9th Street, Philadelphia, PA 19147
Visit website ↗Get directions ↗
8

Blue Corn

MexicanBella Vista$$
Blue Corn

A full-service Mexican restaurant and bar known for handmade blue corn tortillas and a wide menu spanning ceviche, huaraches, and tortas. The al pastor tacos with pineapple and Oaxaca cheese are the crowd favorite.

Signature  Blue corn tacos al pastor
Address: 940 S. 9th Street, Philadelphia, PA 19147
Visit website ↗Get directions ↗
9

Angelo's Pizzeria

PizzaBella Vista$$
Angelo's Pizzeria

Locals line up for Angelo's Sicilian and round pies, hoagies, and one of the city's most praised cheesesteaks on a fresh-baked roll. It's cash-only and worth the wait that regularly stretches down 9th Street.

Signature  Cheesesteak on a housemade roll
Address: 736 S. 9th Street, Philadelphia, PA 19147
Visit website ↗Get directions ↗
10

Alma del Mar

SeafoodBella Vista$$
Alma del Mar

This bright seafood and brunch spot, featured on Netflix's Queer Eye, leans on fresh fish and Latin-inflected plates. Its lobster Benedict, piled with meat over a poached egg and hollandaise, is the reason weekend crowds gather.

Signature  Lobster Benedict
Address: 1007 S. 9th Street, Philadelphia, PA 19147
