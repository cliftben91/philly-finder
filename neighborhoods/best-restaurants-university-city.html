The Best Restaurants in University City

Eclectic dining around Penn and Drexel, from farm-to-table to standout dumplings.

GPBy the GuidePhilly team · Updated July 2026
Advertisement728 × 90 · Monetag zone

Anchored by Penn and Drexel, University City serves a global, student-and-professor-friendly mix, from a pioneering farm-to-table cafe to some of the city's best dumplings and ramen.

Here are the best restaurants in University City right now.

White Dog Cafe

New AmericanUniversity City$$$
White Dog Cafe

A University City institution since 1983, this quirky, dog-themed spot was championing local and sustainable sourcing long before it was fashionable. The seasonal menu leans on Pennsylvania farms and pairs with a bar pouring regional wines.

Signature  Seasonal local menu
Address: 3420 Sansom St, Philadelphia, PA 19104

2

Co-Op Restaurant & Bar

New AmericanUniversity City$$$
Co-Op Restaurant & Bar

Tucked inside the boutique Study hotel on the Penn-Drexel border, Co-Op takes a polished, vegetable-forward approach to mid-Atlantic cooking. Its seasonal menus make it a reliable choice for a parents-in-town meal.

Signature  Lancaster beet tartare
Address: 20 S 33rd St, Philadelphia, PA 19104
Visit website ↗Get directions ↗
3

Louie Louie

AmericanUniversity City$$$
Louie Louie

This stylish restaurant and lounge inside the Inn at Penn is built for date nights and celebratory dinners. Settle into a plush banquette for oysters and cocktails or a full sit-down meal of carpaccio and trout.

Signature  Rainbow trout amandine
Address: 3611 Walnut St, Philadelphia, PA 19104
Visit website ↗Get directions ↗
4

Walnut Street Cafe

New AmericanUniversity City$$$
Walnut Street Cafe

A bright, refined room turning out polished American comfort food built around organic produce from nearby farms. Its Golden Hour happy hour draws crowds for well-priced cocktails and snacks like whipped ricotta and salmon tartare.

Signature  Whipped ricotta
Address: 2929 Walnut St, Philadelphia, PA 19104
Visit website ↗Get directions ↗
5

Sang Kee Noodle House

ChineseUniversity City$$
Sang Kee Noodle House

The University City outpost of Philadelphia's beloved Sang Kee name serves comforting Cantonese cooking inside the Sheraton hotel. It is a go-to for hand-pulled wonton noodle soups and its signature roast duck.

Signature  Duck wonton noodle soup
Address: 3549 Chestnut St, Philadelphia, PA 19104

6

Dim Sum House

ChineseUniversity City$$
Dim Sum House

This moody, leather-boothed space brings both Shanghai and Cantonese dim sum to the heart of campus. Expect elevated small plates like truffle edamame dumplings, shrimp siu mai, and honey-vinegar spare ribs at lunch or dinner.

Signature  Truffle edamame dumplings
Address: 3939 Chestnut St, Philadelphia, PA 19104

7

Terakawa Ramen

JapaneseUniversity City$$
Terakawa Ramen

A pared-down noodle shop staying busy year-round with steaming bowls of shoyu, miso, and tan tan ramen. Beyond the noodles, the kitchen turns out izakaya standards like karaage and takoyaki.

Signature  Tonkotsu ramen
Address: 125 S 40th St, Philadelphia, PA 19104
Visit website ↗Get directions ↗
8

LaScala's Fire

Italian-AmericanUniversity City$$
LaScala's Fire

A cozy Italian-American restaurant right on the Drexel campus, known for its wood-fired oven and reimagined red-sauce classics. It draws students, faculty, and visiting families for casual pizzas and hearty pastas.

Signature  Wood-fired pizza
Address: 3200 Chestnut St, Philadelphia, PA 19104
Visit website ↗Get directions ↗
9

Manakeesh Cafe Bakery

LebaneseUniversity City$$
Manakeesh Cafe Bakery

An all-purpose Lebanese cafe and bakery serving everything from fresh-pressed juices and pastries to full mezze spreads. The namesake manakeesh flatbreads, topped with za'atar, lamb, or garlicky chicken, are the reason to visit.

Signature  Za'atar manakeesh
Address: 4420 Walnut St, Philadelphia, PA 19104

10

Sabrina's Cafe

BrunchUniversity City$$
Sabrina's Cafe

Weekend waits are a rite of passage at this brunch favorite set in a striking Victorian building near 34th and Powelton. The kitchen is famous for its overstuffed challah French toast, tofu scrambles, and loaded tater tots.

Signature  Stuffed challah French toast
Address: 227 N 34th St, Philadelphia, PA 19104

Don Barriga

MexicanUniversity City$$
Don Barriga

A welcome Mexican addition to a corner of the city where the cuisine is underrepresented, earning praise since it opened. Days bring tacos, tortas, and chilaquiles, while dinner centers on sizzling fajitas and the shareable parrillada.

Signature  Parrillada mixed grill
