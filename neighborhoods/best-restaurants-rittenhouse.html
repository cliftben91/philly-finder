The Best Restaurants in Rittenhouse

Philadelphia's most elegant square, ringed by brasseries, fine dining, and buzzy hotel restaurants.

GPBy the GuidePhilly team · Updated July 2026

Rittenhouse Square sits at the center of Philadelphia's most polished dining district, where sidewalk brasseries, hotel dining rooms, and several of the city's best fine-dining tables cluster within a few blocks.

Here are the best restaurants in Rittenhouse right now.

1

Parc

FrenchRittenhouse$$$
Parc

This bustling Parisian-style brasserie anchors the east side of Rittenhouse Square and stays packed from morning coffee through late-night dinner. Its sidewalk seating and classic bistro fare make it one of the neighborhood's defining see-and-be-seen destinations.

Signature  Steak frites
Address: 227 S 18th St, Philadelphia, PA 19103

2

Vernick Food & Drink

New AmericanRittenhouse$$$$
Vernick Food & Drink

Chef Greg Vernick's James Beard Award-winning townhouse restaurant turns pristine seasonal ingredients into refined, ingredient-forward plates. The warm, understated dining room has long been considered one of Philadelphia's finest tables.

Signature  Uni toast
Address: 2031 Walnut St, Philadelphia, PA 19103
Visit website ↗Get directions ↗
3

Friday Saturday Sunday

New AmericanRittenhouse$$$$
Friday Saturday Sunday

A revived 1970s institution, this intimate spot pairs inventive, Michelin-starred cooking with a beloved dining room that helped shape Philadelphia's modern restaurant scene. Its cream of mushroom soup has stayed on the menu for decades and is a genuine city classic.

Signature  Cream of mushroom soup
Address: 261 S 21st St, Philadelphia, PA 19103
Visit website ↗Get directions ↗
4

My Loup

FrenchRittenhouse$$$
My Loup

A stylish modern French restaurant just off the square from the team behind Vernick, serving elegant bistro plates in a lively, art-filled room. It quickly became one of the neighborhood's most talked-about openings and a go-to for a polished night out.

Address: 2005 Walnut St, Philadelphia, PA 19103
Visit website ↗Get directions ↗
5

Her Place Supper Club

New AmericanRittenhouse$$$
Her Place Supper Club

Chef Amanda Shulman runs this reservation-only supper club as a warm, communal dinner party with an ever-changing prix fixe. The cozy, home-kitchen atmosphere and hard-to-get seatings have made it one of Philly's most sought-after tables.

Signature  Nightly set menu
Address: 1740 Sansom St, Philadelphia, PA 19103
Visit website ↗Get directions ↗
6

Wilder

New AmericanRittenhouse$$$
Wilder

Set inside a striking former dance academy, Wilder delivers elevated American cooking across multiple stylish levels near the square. Its wood-fired kitchen and handsome bar make it a versatile choice for both dinner and drinks.

Address: 2009 Sansom St, Philadelphia, PA 19103
Visit website ↗Get directions ↗
7

The Love

AmericanRittenhouse$$$
The Love

A cheerful, farmhouse-chic restaurant from Aimee Olexy and Stephen Starr serving comforting seasonal American plates just steps from Rittenhouse Square. Its sunlit dining room and hearth-driven cooking make it a reliable favorite from brunch through dinner.

Address: 130 S 18th St, Philadelphia, PA 19103
Visit website ↗Get directions ↗
8

Oyster House

SeafoodRittenhouse$$$
Oyster House

This longtime raw bar carries on a family seafood legacy with daily-shucked oysters, snapper soup, and a classic lobster roll. The bright, tiled dining room and happy-hour oyster deals keep it a dependable Center City staple.

Signature  Daily-shucked oysters
Address: 1516 Sansom St, Philadelphia, PA 19102
Visit website ↗Get directions ↗
9

Lacroix

FrenchRittenhouse$$$$
Lacroix

The Rittenhouse Hotel's flagship restaurant overlooks the square and offers refined French-inspired fine dining in a serene, upscale setting. Its lavish Sunday brunch is a longstanding special-occasion tradition among Philadelphians.

Signature  Sunday brunch buffet
Address: 210 W Rittenhouse Sq, Philadelphia, PA 19103
Visit website ↗Get directions ↗
10

Little Water

SeafoodRittenhouse$$$
Little Water

A contemporary coastal restaurant tucked on a quiet block near the square, focused on thoughtfully sourced fish and vegetable-forward plates. Its intimate room and creative crudo have earned it strong critical praise.

Signature  Crudo
Address: 261 S 20th St, Philadelphia, PA 19103
Visit website ↗Get directions ↗
11

Dear Daphni

MediterraneanRittenhouse$$$
Dear Daphni

On the ground floor of The Laurel, this Michael Schulson restaurant offers a modern, shareable take on Mediterranean cooking built around mezze and grilled dishes. Its glamorous, buzzy dining room makes it a popular pick for group dinners near the square.

Address: 125 S 20th St, Philadelphia, PA 19103
